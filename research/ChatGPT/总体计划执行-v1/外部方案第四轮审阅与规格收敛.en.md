# Fourth review: freeze the usable core and close local gaps

[English](外部方案第四轮审阅与规格收敛.en.md) | [简体中文](外部方案第四轮审阅与规格收敛.md)

This historical stage-1 review covers three frozen deliveries, 84,722 bytes and 1,337 text lines, on 2026-10-28. DeepSeek's takeover and limited reuse revision and WorkBuddy's four-part patch contain useful corrections, but neither supplies new measured retrieval gains. I freeze correct components rather than request another expanded architecture essay.

The accepted core separates exact-result identity from compatible-entry lookup, checks full fields after hashing, ranks by current score with stable IDs, permits neighbors in results, and distinguishes Q cache entries, C seeds, M edges and E expanded seeds. Rt, failure event, risk allowance and acceptance rate remain separate. WorkBuddy retains an arbitrary-precision reference, defers nonidentity OPQ and the 128-bit fast path, persists external objects before publication, binds operation IDs to request digests, and introduces reservations and Pending state.

A static snapshot does not make risk prediction impossible. With fixed vectors x=(1,0), y=(0,1), seed {x}, K=1, q1=(1,0) succeeds with seed score 1 while q2=(0,1) fails with seed score 0. Freshness features are constant but observable query scores vary. This disproves a universal impossibility claim, not demonstrates practical predictability. One FiQA negative result cannot universally invalidate similarity matching or establish probe priority. A common fixed corpus also does not automatically make independently sampled deterministic query failures dependent; the actual sampling assumptions remain unproved.

I remove the proposed daily `T′≥U → RETURN_EXACT`. Equality does not settle ID ties: two identical unit vectors with IDs 1/2, a seed containing only ID 2 and K=1 give T′=U=1 but the correct winner is ID 1. An upper bound over the entire corpus, including scored items, is generally at least its best score and rarely useful against a Kth threshold. Useful exclusion concerns unscored regions. The cited v0.14 checks validate another anchor/cap formula and cannot certify this new partition formula or all floating implementations.

Matching and completion still need deterministic candidate selection and real costs. Probe vector fetch/validation is not free unless it is genuinely cached and its storage/build cost is counted. ANN top-K does not supply exact old top-C for free. Reserve before each seed/edge action; an outer edge-budget check can overshoot inside a two-edge loop. Deduplicate pending neighbor IDs before scoring, preserve current-query scores, and use the parent's remaining budget for fallback. Rechecking final K items cannot repair mixed-version candidate omissions; retain one readable snapshot. A newer snapshot does not invalidate a still-pinned historical one.

Returned ANN candidates are not all internal ANN accesses, so 50 outputs cannot prove a fourfold total-read advantage over 200 seed scores. Hypothetical .10/.20 ms or 4.7× examples do not eliminate a route. Compare real deployable quality/cost points and tail behavior, with ordinary ANN-result and exact-result caches where applicable. Compatibility hits are not rewrite repetition rates. Data-dependent exits must be preregistered rather than treated as universal impossibility results.

The zero-failure examples yield 11.73%, 6.05% and 4.95% upper bounds for n=24,48,59. Count independent accepted validation cases, not corpus chunks or every family by default. Data used to select a threshold cannot be merged back into an independent validation count merely because the test set was untouched. Current sample insufficiency is not a theorem against all future methods.

WorkBuddy's reduced-coordinate scale must be `S_reduced*2^(−2*(149−h_min))`. At x=1,q=0,h_min=126, `2^46*2^-46=1`, whereas the opposite sign gives 2^92. Arbitrary precision remains the reference. The proposed γ_2D/Neumaier treatment lacks a complete argument for subtraction, squaring, accumulation and rounding the corrections themselves. A later nextafter cannot repair every upstream unsafe operation. Exact integer truth still incurs nonnegative enclosure error when converted upward to FP64, with scale 2^-298 accounted for.

Cancellation must not refund unknown in-flight I/O. With budget 1000, consumed 600 and issued 300, refunding it as zero and issuing another 300 may execute 1200 total. Distinguish unissued, confirmed canceled, in-flight and completed work; logical requested bytes are charged at issue, and uncertain work remains reserved or conservatively charged. Memory occupancy and cumulative I/O require different ledger semantics.

POSIX rename/directory-fsync assumptions do not establish Windows power-loss recovery. SQLite WAL+FULL is an explicit choice, not a universal default or protection from media damage. I keep publication authority, exact-reference mathematics and shared accounting while deferring unsupported guarantees. At this stopping point labels were unreviewed and stage 2 was not started; the next step was the common execution skeleton, not training or production replacement.
