# Stage 3.1: does cached-candidate reuse pay for itself?

[English](阶段三首部分报告.en.md) | [简体中文](阶段三首部分报告.md)

I completed the first controlled candidate-reuse matrix on 2026-10-29. The outcome is negative for the tested routes: cached seeds alone do not meet the quality floor, and merging seeds with fresh ANN candidates adds overhead without reducing native ANN search work.

The experiment keeps the 1,213-chunk BGE-M3 snapshot, uses 16 anchor families and 48 existing query texts, and performs no new embedding calls. Each trace contains an anchor, rewrite 1, its repetition, rewrite 2 and its repetition. Three replays cover 20 configurations and 80 trace requests, producing 4,800 measured searches. There are another 960 ordinary warmups and 576 paid candidate fills: 6,336 total searches. Repetitions are not independent new queries.

The matrix includes ANN50, full reference, seed-only and merged routes for C=10/50/200 with ANN or exact old-query fill, and enhanced fresh ANN comparisons. Top-1 old-query matching is performed in the compatible bank, excludes self and does not use family labels. A 16-entry candidate bank and common 32-entry ordinary LRU make the constructed workload explicit. Exact old top-C is a diagnostic, not an oracle for the new query.

| Route | Rewrite top-10 overlap | Mean latency / other evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ANN50 | 100% | About 4.179 ms; 606.625 internal distance evaluations on average |
| ANN-seed C10 | 30.9375%; minimum 10% | Fails the fixed quality floor |
| ANN-seed C50 | 80.625%; minimum 20% | Fails the fixed quality floor |
| ANN-seed C200 | 96.25%; minimum 70% | About 8.043 ms; still fails |
| Merge C10/C50/C200 | 100% | About 7.496/7.652/8.361 ms; native work remains 606.625 |

The gate requires minimum and P10 overlap of at least 90%. Average coverage cannot excuse a bad tail. Overlap is measured against complete canonical scoring, not semantic labels, which remain unreviewed.

The main avoidable overhead is JSON cache decoding and matching: approximately 3.44–3.96 ms, compared with about .293 ms of native ANN search in the measured decomposition. This identifies a layout problem worth fixing, not a hidden warm-start benefit. The merged route still runs a normal native search; only candidate sets are combined.

Ten fictitious checks cover persisted selection/validation, scoring continuation, funded empty-cache fallback, unsupported native caps, scope compatibility, common source/commit costs, self-only buckets, invalid IDs/versions, canonical ties and snapshots. The ledger includes cache read/fill work, canonical scores, source access and commits. Forced repeated requests and anchor warmup do not establish a natural cache-hit rate.

I retain the negative results and stop the frozen parameter matrix. The next limited question is whether a compact layout removes the engineering bottleneck and whether an explicitly controllable IVF frontier can test true seed-guided search. This report does not activate learned gating, prefetch, certification, expert training or a production replacement.
