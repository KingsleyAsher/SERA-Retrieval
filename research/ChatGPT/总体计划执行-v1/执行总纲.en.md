# SERA execution plan

[English](执行总纲.en.md) | [简体中文](执行总纲.md)

Status date: 2026-10-29. I retain the original six-stage research program and use this document as its current execution anchor. Earlier paused or unstarted entries describe their historical dates, not the current state.

My objective is a retrieval architecture that improves semantic quality, response time, total resource cost, freshness and stability together. The central hypothesis is that a shared version/dependency state and resource ledger can reduce repeated work across retrieval, candidate reuse, update repair and predictive actions. Ordinary caches and compact layouts must also be available to the baselines.

I start with a personal knowledge base and will later investigate larger real corpora. Original document size alone does not describe index workload: I also record chunk count, dimension, partition balance, filtering, query/update patterns and index size. Results on 1,213 chunks cannot establish performance for a very large database.

The daily retrieval path and exact-certification branch are separate. BGE-M3 dense1024 remains the embedding contract; expert-model training is paused. Private originals are read-only, and documents and credentials stay outside public artifacts. Lightweight CPU calibration is allowed when there is independent evidence to train and calibrate it.

| Stage | Deliverable and acceptance | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Correct proposals; freeze data, metrics and family-level splits; review relevance labels | Technical assets ready; labels unreviewed |
| 2 | Common request/state/action/result/ledger interfaces with full, ANN and Chinese hybrid backends | Delivered; counterexample-driven repairs verified |

| 4 | Independent bounds, numerical reference, verifier, snapshot publication and resumable exact path | Theory preparation; formal implementation pending |
| 5 | Joint mechanism against individual components and equally equipped strong baselines | Not started |
| 6 | Real deduplicated scale-up, recovery, update interleaving and shadow operation | Not started |

I review each completed part before approving the next. Technical preparation does not approve semantic labels. A proposed 20% reduction in one major cost is a challenge rather than a predicted benefit; all other dimensions and failures must remain visible. At least two meaningful workloads need repeatable gains before a combined-prototype review. Correctness, engineering usability, mechanism value, novelty and generational improvement have separate acceptance decisions.

Stage 3.1 showed that cached seeds alone miss results and merging adds cost without reducing native ANN work. Stage 3.2 confirmed a compact-layout benefit and some same-budget coverage gains, but not stable net superiority over strong baselines. Its 44 configurations and 10,560 measured requests are development diagnostics, not independent semantic tests.

The next limited part first checks active partition tails, pending scores and atomic seed accounting, then diagnoses geometric bounds offline. It does not deploy bound-based stopping, train a model, expand the corpus or issue strict certificates. After its report I will decide whether online stopping merits a separate experiment. Failed configurations remain archived; I will not expand the same development grid indefinitely to manufacture a positive result.
