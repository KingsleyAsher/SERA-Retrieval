# Stage 3.3 protocol: unfinished frontiers, paid seeds and offline bounds

[English](下一部分协议.en.md) | [简体中文](下一部分协议.md)

This protocol is prepared but unimplemented as of 2026-10-29. I will first establish continuation/accounting invariants, then diagnose geometric bounds offline. This part does not deploy bound-based stopping, issue probability or exact certificates, train a stop model, expand the corpus or enlarge the parameter grid. Online stopping requires a later decision after this report.

The review handoff used an older status. Actual completed work is stage 3.2. That executor already charges `canonical_scores` and `vector_logical_bytes` before canonical batches: IVF16 warm C200/B256 averages 200 seed scores plus 56 new scores, with 2,097,152 vector bytes. The external seed-accounting defect is a prevention requirement, not evidence that those results were uncharged. Likewise, an upper-bound heap omission in pseudocode is not an observed defect in the existing non-pruning backend.

## State invariants

`completed_partitions` means every legal member is covered by a valid current-query score. Pruned partitions are recorded separately. `active_partition` identifies the unfinished list, `enumeration_cursor` its next posting, and `pending_positions+score_cursor` the enumerated-but-unscored work and completed prefix. `scored` is unique by relevant ID/version and score contract. `order_frontier` schedules visits; `coverage_frontier` represents every unfinished region, including unopened lists, active tails and pending work. `limits+consumed` belong to the same request across all continuations and fallbacks. Equivalent data structures are allowed; two physical heaps are not mandatory.

Taking a partition records it as active, not completed. Enumerating an ID puts it in pending; scoring and retaining its result moves it to scored. A popped bound entry cannot vanish from unfinished coverage while its partition is active. Completion requires all legal members covered and no pending work. Previously scored seeds are skipped without rereading their vectors. Budget exhaustion preserves active state, both cursors, pending scores and historical consumption. Even an empty ordering queue cannot establish completion if an active tail or pending item remains.

The primary counterexample has seed score .5 and a list with .6 followed by .95. An interruption after .6 must still reach .95 on resume. Also test enumeration before any score, the last enumerated but unscored member and lists entirely covered by seeds. These are normal recorded-checkpoint semantics, not physical exactly-once execution across a crash between computation and persistence.

## Resource admission

Atomically admit every implemented hard dimension before a new score. At dimension 1024, one float64 vector is 8192 bytes; its 16-byte ID/version metadata is not a vector read. `canonical_scores = seed_scores + new_scan_scores + other_new_scores` counts actual unique new scores. Reusing an exactly compatible retained score avoids recomputation but still pays its lookup and dependency checks.

Record matching, Δ and norms, bound reads/building, heap work, source access, commits, setup, filling and invalidation separately. Duplicate posting checks pay metadata work but not a duplicate vector score. Unsupported hard counters are rejected. If any hard allowance is insufficient, none of the action's other dimensions are deducted. Different ablation arms have independent ledgers under the same rules; a fallback shares its own parent's remaining allowance. Extra checkpoint cost is additional, while successful scoring/read totals should match uninterrupted execution.

## First gate: ten fixed checks

1. Preserve the .5/.6/.95 tail through interruption and a new Engine.
2. Persist enumeration interruptions separately from scoring interruptions.
3. Do not complete a list whose final member remains unscored.
4. With zero/one available seed score, admit score and vector-byte counters atomically; resume without missing charges.
5. Score seed/posting duplicates once and reconcile component counts.
6. Preserve unfinished coverage when interruption follows a queue or bound-heap pop.
7. Exhaust N<K legal items and return N; finish an empty range normally.
8. Distinguish deliberate fixed-budget approximate completion from hard-budget Pending; avoid nonprogressing Pending.
9. Reject changed-scope/changed-snapshot coverage while allowing compatible continuation.
10. At full range, match canonical scoring and retain source/commit actions.

If a core invariant fails, repair and record it before integrating diagnostics with the executor. An existing invariant may be demonstrated without rewriting working code.

## Frozen offline matrix

Use the same 1,213 chunks, revision 1216 and BGE-M3 dense1024 coordinates. Use only 16 existing anchors and 32 rewrites, with no new API calls or sealed-test access. Build 16/64 lists using KMeans n_init=1, seed 20261029, max_iter=50, tol=1e-4 and Lloyd's algorithm. Stage 3.2 did not persist complete layout hashes, so freeze assignments, centers, postings and statistics now; identical algorithm parameters do not prove identical physical reuse.

Use M=16 contiguous orthogonal 64-dimensional subspaces, identity coordinates, no OPQ and no M search. C=50/200 support pools come from paid actual ANN fills; different C pools are not assumed nested. Stable top-1 current-query inner-product matching excludes self and never uses family labels. The main scope is all currently visible items with no source/date filter. Scope expansion appears only in the fictitious rejection tests. There are 32×2×2=128 diagnostic cases, not 128 independent queries.

The daily score remains `normalized_ip_f64_v1`, K=10 and ascending ID on true ties. No rerounding to FP32 or relabeling via `2(1-s)` creates a stored-FP32 certificate. Same snapshot/model/preprocessing/score/filter compatibility is required. Support legality and bound membership coverage are distinct dependencies.

## Geometry and numerical boundary

Each block records its membership fingerprint, center c, radius ρ, subspace offset-norm ranges lo/hi, maximum member norm β and maximum squared norm νmax. Support records include legal distinct IDs/versions and their query/scoring dependencies, not only a scalar T0.

In real arithmetic:

```text
LB_sphere = max(||q-c||-ρ,0)^2
LB_stat = Σ gap(||(q-c)_m||,[lo_m,hi_m])^2
LB_combined = max(LB_sphere,LB_stat)
U_ball = q·c + ||q||ρ
U_from_L2 = (||q||^2 + νmax - LB)/2
U_migrated = U0 + βΔ
T_support_lower = T0 - βsupport Δ
```

The general center need not be unit norm. νmax must cover every member rather than be replaced with 1. The minimum of valid IP upper bounds remains valid. K legal supports with old scores at least T0 establish the migrated Kth-score lower bound; they need not be the old global exact top-K. If rescoring already provides the current threshold, use it instead of an unnecessarily weaker old threshold. A migrated bound is not universally looser than every independently chosen fresh bound.

Exclusion requires strict separation; overlapping intervals do not prove order or equality. Ordinary float64 observations are named `U_observed/T_observed`, not certified `U_upper/T_lower`. Record raw bound violations and gaps without an arbitrary epsilon that hides them. A finite zero-violation sample is not a universal numerical proof. Preserve a private counterexample for any violation and bar that unproved path from online guarantees. The exact FP32 branch is independent.

## Metrics, attribution and exit

Report each query/block's size, unscored members, bound/threshold gaps, sphere-only/stat-only/both/neither categories with all legal blocks as denominator, unique potentially excluded items, planning time, logical reads, metadata payload and matching/support cost. Already paid seed scores cannot be claimed as avoided future work. Keep whole-block exclusion counts separate from unique still-unscored avoidable counts, whose screening denominator is the full legal range.

Separate a legally constructible support threshold from an offline exact top-K oracle. Enumeration for evaluation has its own ledger and is not free online work. State build/storage/update dependencies and unmeasured maintenance. Do not replace top10 with top20, block hits or fewer posting checks, and do not infer semantic uplift from unreviewed labels.

Any later online test must distinguish A=no seeds/cold order/fixed budget; B=no seeds/cold order/bounds; C=paid seeds/cold order/bounds; D=same seeds/seed order/bounds; E=same seeds/seed order/fixed total budget. C−B measures the joint seed addition, D−C isolates order, D−E isolates stopping. Equal nprobe is not equal total score work. Mature ANN, equally equipped cold IVF and full scoring stay in the comparison; dominated cold points cannot substantiate gains. This table does not authorize those online runs in this part.

Completion means passing the first gate and reporting reproducible 128-case diagnostics with interpretable costs, even if bounds have no useful pruning potential. A proposed lead for a later online investigation is at least one fixed configuration with legal current supports and at least 8/32 rewrites whose still-unscored excludable members reach 20% of the entire legal range. Those are screening proposals, not statistical guarantees or savings promises. If absent, record and shelve the configuration rather than grow the development grid. After the report I decide the next step; no training, production change or strict certificate is implied.
