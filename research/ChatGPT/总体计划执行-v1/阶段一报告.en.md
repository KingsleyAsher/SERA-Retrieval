# Stage 1 report

[English](阶段一报告.en.md) | [简体中文](阶段一报告.md)

On 2026-10-28 I completed the technical preparation for the new execution plan: proposal corrections, a frozen benchmark, metric definitions, split rules and small counterexample checks. I did not implement the shared executor or a strict-certification system in this part.

The frozen private snapshot contains 1,213 chunks from 114 sources at revision 1216, with BGE-M3 dense1024 vectors. The question draft has 120 families and 360 formulations, divided 72/24/24 by family. Labels remain provisional. A fixed 12-family development audit is prepared; approval to continue engineering is not approval of those labels.

I retained combined geometric bounds and per-vector residual reasoning as candidates, but rejected interval-overlap tie breaking, unaccounted OPQ distortion, recovery from uncommitted manifests and prediction-based hard-budget claims. For candidate reuse I retained rescore-and-complete, while separating external candidate merging from an actual search warm start. Changed trajectories require independent calibration.

The necessary checks cover exact-versus-rounded ties, nonorthogonal distortion, current-query thresholds, committed publication authority, budget prediction versus actual admission, unjudged-label handling and metrics with known answers. These are small correctness probes, not crash recovery, concurrency, large-scale performance or a completed numerical certificate.

The result of this stage is a reviewable protocol and technical baseline. It provides no new acceleration percentage, no verified semantic uplift and no novelty conclusion. At this historical stopping point stage 2 had not started. Later progress is recorded in the current roadmap, not retroactively attributed to stage 1.
