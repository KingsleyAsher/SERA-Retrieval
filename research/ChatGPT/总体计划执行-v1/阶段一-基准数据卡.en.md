# Benchmark data card

[English](阶段一-基准数据卡.en.md) | [简体中文](阶段一-基准数据卡.md)

I froze the first benchmark on 2026-10-28 using an isolated copy, not a mutable view of the original knowledge base. Revision 1216 contains 1,213 chunks from 114 sources. Embeddings are normalized float64, dimension 1024, under the fixed BGE-M3 namespace. Chunking uses a 900-character target and 90-character overlap. Vector and metadata hashes identify the private assets.

The daily contract is `normalized_ip_f64_v1`, with ascending internal ID for equal scores. The future `stored_fp32_real_sq_l2_v1` certification contract is distinct; I have not converted this benchmark into that contract.

The question draft comprises 120 families with three formulations each. Six categories have 20 families apiece: factual lookup, reformulation, cross-document combinations, source/time filtering, unanswerable cases and update scenarios. Each category contributes 12/4/4 families to development/calibration/test, giving 72/24/24 overall. Main sources do not cross splits; secondary sources for combination questions stay inside their split.

These are title/chunk-anchored, template-assisted drafts. They are not 120 validated natural questions or complete relevance gold. Seed qrels identify initially selected chunks; unjudged documents are not automatically irrelevant. Combination drafts require both source sides without inventing a factual relationship between them.

Twenty unanswerable families use fictitious unique identifiers whose absence still needs review. Twenty update families define scenarios rather than executed mutations and are excluded from static quality scoring. One time-filter subcase lacks reliable update metadata and is explicitly unavailable. Ninety of 114 sources have parsed update dates; document, event and index-commit time remain different concepts.

My fixed audit sample takes two development families from each category, 12 total. If substantive errors appear, I will revise that class and review another fixed 12-family sample. Engineering continuation does not count as label acceptance. The 24 sealed test families have not been used for embedding, training, calibration or retrieval evaluation at this stage. Filesystem separation is a workflow measure, not a security claim.

Quality metrics include nDCG@10, pooled relevant-document recall, source consistency and unanswerable behavior. Exact-vector top-10 overlap is reported separately. Incomplete judgment pools cannot support exhaustive semantic recall. Costs distinguish model/network, loading, search, persistence, source reads, caching, setup, updates and fallback. The baseline plan includes full scoring, mature ANN, Chinese lexical/vector hybrid and equally equipped cached/incremental combinations. Formal quality conclusions remain blocked by unreviewed labels.
