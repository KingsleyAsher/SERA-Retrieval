# Admission decisions for the initial proposals

[English](阶段一-方案修正清单.en.md) | [简体中文](阶段一-方案修正清单.md)

I reviewed the WorkBuddy MOG Sigma v3 proposal and DeepSeek's first follow-up as research inputs on 2026-10-28. Their suggestions do not independently authorize execution. The source identities and hashes remain in the archived Chinese record and machine evidence.

For WorkBuddy I retain the maximum of valid sphere and centered-subspace squared-L2 lower bounds, explicit result states, snapshot binding, per-vector reconstruction residuals and resource accounting. A residual bound has the form `max(sqrt(d_pq)-r,0)^2`: clipping occurs before squaring. Reconstruction, coordinates, transform error and numerical enclosure must use compatible semantics. Identity rotation is the first reference path.

Overlapping intervals do not establish equality. The candidates q=(0,0), x=(1,0), ID 2 and y=(1,2^-50), ID 1 have exact squared distances 1 and `1+2^-100`; both coordinates are FP32-representable, while ordinary FP64 accumulation may tie. Exact dyadic comparison is required before using IDs. Neumaier or double-double arithmetic alone is not an enclosure proof.

An unaccounted transform can invalidate a bound: with R=1.01, q=2, x=1 and reconstruction zero, subtracting the original residual from transformed distance yields 1.0404 after squaring, above the true distance 1. Hashes identify components but do not prevent time-of-check/time-of-use errors or mixed generations. Immutable objects, query pins and committed publication records must supply consistency. The highest valid manifest may still be uncommitted; SQLite's committed record is authoritative. A numeric epoch alone does not fence writers.

P90 cost predictions schedule work; actual counters admit it. A deadline cannot instantly cancel already issued I/O, and replaying logical decisions does not reproduce wall-clock cancellation. Multi-producer tests must respect the single-writer protocol. Claimed literature gaps and 10M/100M or P99 targets are research questions, not established capabilities.

For DeepSeek I retain legal cached-candidate rescoring plus newly retrieved candidates. A threshold is not a backend entry point, queue, visited set or continuation state. USearch's public search interface does not expose those controls; genuine warm-start experiments need a controllable backend. DARTH-family guarantees cannot be inherited after changing trajectories or training conditions.

If a current-query distance threshold T(q) is available, the sufficient exclusion check is `L0-Δ>T(q)`. Reusing an old-radius half-gap formula is generally unnecessarily conservative, not automatically the tight condition. Quality target, confidence level, retained fraction, replacement fraction and full-fallback rate need separate definitions. Similar-entry lookup has real distance/index cost rather than universal O(1) behavior.

Deleted items cannot be returned. New items need the current index or an explicit delta channel; lazy insertion is a separately declared freshness policy. A fully matching snapshot requirement cannot simultaneously claim uncoordinated cross-version reuse. Candidate validity and navigation/coverage freshness are separate dependencies.

Literature numbers remain attributed author reports: Aker reports up to 64 percentage points, 3.2× QPS and 0.6× memory; these are not reproduced SERA benefits. The cited DARTH+ 0.2% figure and the alleged 1536-dimensional/50K sphere exclusion of 0.2% were not accepted as verified facts. FreshCache's temporal risk is not nearest-neighbor omission risk. LiveVectorLake's reported 100-document/five-time-point case does not establish arbitrary-update guarantees. SIFT/GIST may support geometry diagnostics but cannot replace the Chinese BGE-M3 target data.

Unresolved guarantee mechanisms stay outside the guarantee path. A negative window narrows that configuration rather than disproving all reuse. This review did not implement certification or run a new retrieval benchmark.
