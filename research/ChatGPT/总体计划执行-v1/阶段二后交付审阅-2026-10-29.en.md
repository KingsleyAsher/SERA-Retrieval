| Finding | Repair |

All seven probe predicates change from defect-present to defect-absent. The 12 existing fictitious checks and four added boundary checks pass. The latter cover resuming paid prepare work in a new Engine, cache-return admission, copied-store checkpoint rejection and nondefault hybrid contracts. These are tiny SQLite mechanics checks, not power-loss, concurrency or strict-certification evidence.

Snapshot semantics matter: a request pins its starting snapshot; it need not become the latest version at response completion. Text hashes detect stored-text changes when the stored digest is not updated; they are not protection against an adversary rewriting both. Path-bound checkpoints do not silently migrate when a store is moved.

The new history schema requires `content_sha`. Old run-001/run-002 databases are preserved and are not automatically migrated; replay them with saved code or explicitly import into a fresh isolated store. Historical timing, including the reported 3.156 ms ANN value, belongs to the pre-repair code. It has not been remeasured for the repaired version. Counts accumulate across resumes, whereas `seconds` measures the current invocation; sum invocation times without summing cumulative counters again.

I retain DeepSeek's Q/C separation, C=10/50/200, top-1 old-query matching, common ordinary LRU, paid ANN/exact fill, same-snapshot scope and approximate daily results. Admission requires one parent budget across reuse/new search/fallback/source/checkpoint/commit, persisted reuse progress, terminating empty and insufficient-K cases, and full cost disclosure. Exact old top-C is not an oracle upper bound on new-query coverage. Q=16 anchor warmup is a paid controlled workload, not natural hit-rate evidence. Quality and cost pass independently; a cheap route that misses the quality floor does not pass.

At this historical stop, stage 2 was repaired, stage 3 awaited my go-ahead, and semantic labels remained unreviewed. No original knowledge-base data, training directory, embedding API or production path was changed by the repair part.
