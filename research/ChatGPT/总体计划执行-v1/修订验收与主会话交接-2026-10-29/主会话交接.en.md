I use this record to connect the independent review stream to implementation. It concerns WorkBuddy's limited combined-bound revision and DeepSeek's sixth warm-start revision. The review covers two sources totaling 27,224 bytes and 468 lines, with eight constructed checks. Those checks establish local counterexamples and specification constraints; they are not a new benchmark or proof that the current backend exhibits every pseudocode defect.

The usable mathematical core includes the general-center ball formula, reverse-triangle subspace bounds, a maximum of valid lower bounds, and separate online support versus offline oracle thresholds. The two bounds do not universally dominate one another. Refining overlapping intervals is normal; IDs resolve only true equality. Diagnostics must use an explicit denominator over all eligible blocks.

For warm starts, seed scores can initialize a legal threshold and guide order. A valid IP ball upper bound is `q·c+||q||ρ` even for a nonunit center. With Δ=||q−q0|| and suitable member/support norm caps, a previous upper bound becomes `U0+βΔ`, while K distinct current-legal supports with old scores at least T0 imply a new Kth-score lower bound `T0−βsupportΔ`. Old global exact top-K is unnecessary. There is no universal .99-similarity or fixed-Δ validity window.

The primary execution constraint is an interrupted partial partition: a .5 seed followed by list scores .6 and .95 must not lose .95 after stopping at .6. Marking a whole list visited/completed too early or removing its only coverage entry on heap pop can lose the active tail. Preserve active partition, member cursor, pending work, scores and global unfinished coverage; scheduling state alone is insufficient.

Seed accounting must charge vector access and current-query scoring, not only a 16-byte ID/version lookup. At dimension 1024 float64, each actual new vector score reads 8192 logical bytes. Atomic multi-resource admission, duplicate handling, source and commit costs remain shared with fallback. The existing stage-3.2 executor already charges those score/read totals, so this is a pseudocode correction and regression constraint, not a finding that its prior numbers are free.

Filtering has two dependencies: support validity and block membership coverage. Validating returned seeds alone does not make old block bounds valid after scope expansion. Migrated bounds also need conditional comparison with fresh bounds; they are not automatically looser under arbitrary center/radius constructions.

A warm-versus-cold comparison that changes seeds, thresholds and ordering cannot isolate order. A same-seeds cold-order comparator is necessary; equal nprobe does not equal total vector budget. Keep K=10, valid list-count parameters, exhaustive completion for N<K and a clear distinction between fixed-budget approximate completion and resumable hard exhaustion. The 606.625 distance count is an average, not an integer per-query constant.

The implementation decision is to turn these conditions into a finite protocol, not copy the external pseudocode. This handoff's older stage reference is historical; the current execution plan remains authoritative. No new measured speedup, full floating-point proof or production guarantee follows from accepting it.
