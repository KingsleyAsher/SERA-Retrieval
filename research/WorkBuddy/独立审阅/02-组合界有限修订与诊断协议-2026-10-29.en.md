# WorkBuddy proposal record: combined-bound revision and diagnosis

[English](02-组合界有限修订与诊断协议-2026-10-29.en.md) | [简体中文](02-组合界有限修订与诊断协议-2026-10-29.md)

I retain this attributed WorkBuddy research input as a proposed mathematical and diagnostic specification, not as a measured SERA improvement. The integration review adds conditions that must be satisfied before guarantee-bearing implementation.

Let each block have a center c and a covering radius ρ in the declared squared-L2 coordinate system. A query has lower bound `max(||q−c||−ρ,0)^2`. With disjoint orthogonal subspaces and all member offset norms in [lo_m,hi_m], the sum of squared gaps between `||(q−c)_m||` and those intervals is also a lower bound. Reverse triangle gives each term; taking the maximum preserves validity. The two bounds can each win on different blocks.

A centroid of unit members generally has norm below one. Use the complete center-distance formula or rebuild all metadata after selecting a normalized center. Do not infer stored-coordinate exact unit norm from the BGE-M3 model name. The 1024-dimensional dense output specification does not determine storage precision, preprocessing or a machine-certified metric.

The intended diagnosis starts with 16/64 blocks and an identity-coordinate decomposition, compares each bound with its combination, and records true block distances for evaluation only. An online threshold comes from K distinct legal current candidates, not an assumed free exact top-K. It may be looser than the oracle without being wrong. Separate thresholds, strict exclusion direction, score-to-distance conversion and stable ID equality semantics.

Diagnostic categories include sphere-only, statistic-only, both and neither, with all eligible blocks as denominator. Record slack/gaps, block size, candidate support, remaining work, metadata payload and build/planning/update costs. An unpruned block can be legitimately near the query or face a weak candidate threshold; that is not automatically an implementation defect. Suggested centroid/residual cutoffs are tunable design values, not model constants.

Overlapping enclosing intervals are inconclusive and should trigger refinement. True equality implies overlap for valid intervals enclosing the same scalar, but overlap does not imply equality. Approximate equality within epsilon is not the exact tie contract. Count refinement cost separately rather than label refinement a correctness failure.

Ordinary FP64 calculations may support empirical tightness diagnostics. A rigorous certificate additionally needs conservative radii, norm ranges, distance evaluation, threshold endpoints and strict comparison. A zero-violation sample is a necessary diagnostic, not proof over all finite inputs. Residual/PQ/OPQ layers remain later options; the block-bound value question comes first.

I use this proposal only under the reviewed coverage, numerical and accounting constraints. Future inner-product diagnostics need valid general-norm conversion, for example `(||q||²+νmax−LB)/2`, not an unjustified exact-unit shortcut. No live pruning or certification result is established by accepting the proposal.
