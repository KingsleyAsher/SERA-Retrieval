# DeepSeek proposal record: limited warm-start candidates

[English](06-热启动候选有限修订-2026-10-29.en.md) | [简体中文](06-热启动候选有限修订-2026-10-29.md)

I retain this attributed DeepSeek input as a proposed route for seed-guided IVF and query-difference bound reuse. It is not the implementation authority: the reviewed handoff and stage-3.3 protocol state the conditions required before integration.

S1 starts from legal cached IDs whose vectors are rescored against the current query. Those observations can initialize a Kth threshold and influence list visitation. New scans skip already scored items. A scheduling score or list-center inner product may prioritize work, but only a valid bound over the full unfinished set can justify stopping. Queued, unscheduled, active and enumerated-but-unscored regions all matter.

A general IP ball bound is `U=q·c+||q||ρ`; the center need not be unit. Stopping needs a supported current threshold and strict separation from every eligible unscored region. K=10 means top10 throughout. An exhausted legal range with fewer than K members can complete with fewer results; deliberate budgeted approximation and resumable hard exhaustion must remain separate states.

S2 carries old bounds to a new query. With Δ=||q−q0|| and member norm cap β, an old block upper bound U0 becomes `U0+βΔ`. K distinct legal support items whose old scores are at least T0 and whose norms are bounded by βsupport imply a new Kth-score lower bound `T0−βsupportΔ`. They need not be the old global exact top-K. If current rescoring already gives a threshold, use that direct value rather than unnecessarily weakening it. Support records must bind IDs, versions, query/scoring and scope.

No universal .99-similarity or fixed-Δ window follows: admissibility depends on actual block/support margins. Migrated versus fresh bounds also need explicit conditional assumptions about centers, radii and how each bound was obtained. A universal statement that every migrated bound is looser than every fresh construction is not accepted.

The reviewed execution counterexample is a .5 seed and a partition containing .6 then .95. Marking the partition visited/completed after the first member loses .95 on continuation. Removing its bound on heap pop has the same risk unless active state retains its coverage. Opening, enumerating and scoring are different facts. Save the active list, member cursor, pending positions, scored set, budget and unfinished coverage together.

Seed IP calculations read vectors and consume scores. An ID/version pair of 16 bytes is not an 8192-byte float64 vector at dimension 1024. Charge matching, norm/Δ work, bounds, metadata, seed reads/scores, new scans, source/commit, construction and maintenance. A fallback inherits the same remaining budget. The existing stage-3.2 executor already accounts for its seed reads/scores; this external pseudocode issue does not erase its recorded evidence.

Scope changes need two checks: support legality and the membership set covered by each bound. Revalidating only seeds cannot authorize reuse after a filter expands. The first diagnostic therefore stays within one snapshot/model/preprocessing/score/filter bucket. Ordinary FP64 observations remain empirical and cannot issue an exact certificate without numerical enclosure proofs.

To isolate mechanisms, compare no-seed cold-fixed, no-seed cold-bound, same-seed cold-bound, same-seed warm-bound and same-seed warm-fixed routes. Warm-versus-no-seed combines initialization, threshold and order; only the same-seed order pair isolates ordering. Same nprobe is not equal total vector work, and list-count parameters must be valid for 16/64 lists. Preserve mature ANN and the best feasible cold configurations.

Any proposal estimates, proposed controls and mathematical statements remain separate from measured project results. There is no new speedup or large-scale evidence in this handoff. I defer online stopping to a separately approved experiment after continuation/accounting checks and offline diagnosis.
