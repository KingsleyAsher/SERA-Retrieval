# Repository writing and research rules

[English](AGENTS.md) | [简体中文](AGENTS.zh-CN.md)

I maintain Chinese and English prose editions as independently written project documents. Start both from the same facts and claims; do not translate one into the other or publish automatic-translation drafts. Use my project perspective and preserve external attribution. Avoid assistant-session narration about a separate requester.

Every new prose document needs a corresponding language entry and navigation. Keep code, numeric data, identifiers, hashes and official license text canonical; explain those artifacts in both language guides. Update the bilingual manifest and index when adding documents. Preserve source/experiment provenance separately from editorial changes.

Do not change research status, metrics, formulas, budgets, model or acceptance criteria during documentation work. Expert training remains paused. Private knowledge-base data and credentials are excluded. Review each completed research part before advancing. Use only necessary checks; do not rerun private experiments for prose edits.
